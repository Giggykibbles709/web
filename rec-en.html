About the Game

Enter Sector 4, a residential complex sealed under strict military quarantine where the truth is not revealed through official reports, but through the distorted lens of your tactical bodycam.

Combining the claustrophobic atmosphere of first-person tactical horror with the raw immersion of found footage, the game puts you in the boots of a rapid-response officer deployed to retrieve classified data after losing contact with the primary containment team.

Every corner counts: a realistic physics-driven camera system records your descent into the nightmare in real time, where limited battery management, scarce ammunition, and environmental paranoia will determine whether you escape with the evidence or become just another casualty of the isolation protocol.
