About the game

Mall-Evolence is a classic survival horror game that transforms a shopping mall into a claustrophobic nightmare. Set during the "Crystal Dawn" chemical incident, the game explores how an experimental agent released through the ventilation system transforms shoppers into consumer-obsessed entities, violently attacking anything they don't consider "merchandise."

In this title, you don't play as a soldier, but as an ordinary worker who must survive with whatever is at hand. With a fixed camera system that heightens the tension, you must manage a limited inventory, collect strategic resources, and engage in brutal and desperate melee combat, where every decision could be your last.

The game recaptures the essence of pure survival horror: cinematic camera angles, manual saves at specific points, and an oppressive atmosphere. Explore the nooks and crannies of a ruined mall, avoid being "acquired" by the infected, and prove that, in the world of mass consumption, your life is the only thing that's priceless.
