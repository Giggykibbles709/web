About the game

Infiltrate the dark secrets of MALL-EVOLENCE, a survival horror that pays a faithful tribute to the golden era of 32-bit gaming.

Join investigative reporter Jade in a harrowing, claustrophobic journey where resource scarcity, strict inventory management, and fixed camera angles will test your survival instincts.

Solve environment-driven puzzles integrated into the scenery, confront the outbreaks of a corrupt corporation under the beam of your flashlight, and discover if you have what it takes to escape the warehouse and uncover the truth.
