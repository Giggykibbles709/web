About the game

Discover an ever-expanding universe of interactive stories.

This game brings together stories from different genres where your choices matter and lead you to different endings. The first story immerses you in a cyberpunk world, amidst neon lights, conspiracies, and moral dilemmas.

New exclusive stories will be added every month, offering you unique experiences ranging from science fiction to mystery, drama, and adventure.
