About the game

OVERDRIVE: SPEED LEGENDS is a 3D racing game with a low-poly art style, where players compete on various tracks to earn money and reputation. Tracks and cars unlock progressively based on your performance.

Race across 5 distinct circuits, each featuring unique styles and adaptive AI. Choose from 5 different cars, each with its own handling and feel, ensuring a true sense of progression as you advance through the game.

The game also includes a profile system, allowing you to create up to 3 different profiles. Customize each one with a name, country, and icon that will appear on the race leaderboards.
