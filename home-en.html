About the game

H.O.M.E. is a narrative game set in a dystopian future where the government of a city controls every aspect of its citizens' lives, cutting off communication with the outside world under a martial law regime imposed after a rebel attack. However, one exception has emerged: the use of letters.

You, as the player, decide to work within this postal network, tasked with filtering letters before they reach their recipients. Your role is critical: will you deliver the letters, allowing human connections to flourish and risking the regime’s stability? Or will you discard them, supporting the authoritarian control?

Every decision you make will impact not only the characters' lives and relationships but also the direction of the story, leading to different endings based on your actions.
