About the game

In Plague of the Inquisiton, a third-person action-adventure game, you play as Ilana, a young doctor caught in a secret resistance movement against the Inquisition. As the city crumbles under the weight of the plague and religious oppression, Ilana embarks on a quest to find a mysterious artifact that could change the fate of the world.

But time is not on her side. Infected by the very disease she’s fighting and hunted by powerful enemies, she must rely on her skills in parkour, stealth, and combat to survive. Explore a richly detailed and atmospheric recreation of 16th-century Seville, a unique setting never before seen in a video game—where every street hides secrets, danger, and choices that will shape the course of the story.
