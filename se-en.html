About the Game

Welcome to San Espada, a metropolis stifled by heat and corruption where morality is not a simple narrative choice, but a living system that reacts to your every maneuver behind the wheel.

Combining the classic aesthetic of top-down action titles with the depth of modern simulation systems, the game grants you absolute freedom to carve your own path: enforce the law as a road vigilante or delve into the shadows of organized crime.

Every decision counts: a dynamic reputation system evaluates your behavior in real-time, altering police aggressiveness, gang reactions, and available contracts as you walk the fine line between becoming the city's hero or its public enemy number one.
