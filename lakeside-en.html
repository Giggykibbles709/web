About the game

In this game you play as the main character who, tired of his job and daily routine, decides to make a change in his life. He buys a van and prepares it to travel and explore the country. Throughout the adventure, you’ll experience peaceful moments by the lakeside as well as tense situations in a dark forest.

You’ll have a certain degree of freedom to drive your van and enjoy various activities to keep yourself entertained while exploring any landscape you can imagine. Along the way, as the protagonist, you’ll face choices and reflect on your life up to the present, turning this journey into an adventure of self-discovery.
