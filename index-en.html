About Me

I'm Manuel Jesús — a game developer and university student, but above all, someone who sees video games as a way to tell stories, spark emotions, and connect with players. I enjoy exploring a variety of genres and styles, from light, arcade experiences to deeper, narrative-driven projects.

Throughout my journey, I’ve brought several games to life that have allowed me to explore different aspects of development. Some of these include H.O.M.E., an interactive dystopia about information control; OVERDRIVE: SPEED LEGENDS, a fast-paced low poly racing arcade; and Cuando Se Enciende La Luz, a short but intense psychological horror experience built in Unreal Engine 5.

I’m currently balancing my studies with the development of new personal projects like Lakeside, a road trip adventure that blends introspection, exploration, and suspense. My goal is simple: to keep growing as a developer and to create games that don’t just play — they resonate.
